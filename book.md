## Printing the booklet

1. Print double-sided with your print dialog's **Booklet** option (in Adobe Acrobat Reader: Print, then Booklet).
2. Fold the stack of sheets in half and staple along the fold.

No Booklet option? Print two pages per side, double-sided and flipped on the short edge, then staple the sheets at the corner instead of folding.
